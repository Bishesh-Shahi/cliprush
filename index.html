ClipRush

ClipRush is a personal desktop tool that turns highlights from live streams into short vertical videos with captions, and uploads them to the operator's own social media accounts (TikTok, YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels) through each platform's official API.

How it works

  1. It records a live stream on the operator's own computer and watches public chat activity to find highlight moments.
  2. It cuts each highlight into a 9:16 video with captions.
  3. With the operator's permission (OAuth login), it uploads the video to the operator's own account. On TikTok, videos are sent to the creator's inbox as drafts, and the creator reviews, edits and posts them in the TikTok app.

Only content from streamers who allow clipping is used, and every post credits the original stream.
