Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

ClipRush is a personal desktop tool. It has no servers, user accounts or analytics, and it does not sell or share data.

What the app accesses

  • Account authorization: when the owner connects TikTok, YouTube or Instagram, the app receives access tokens from that platform. The scopes used are limited to basic profile info and uploading videos (for TikTok: user.info.basic and video.upload).
  • Videos: clips created on the owner's computer, which the app uploads to the owner's own account.
  • Public stream data: public live stream video and public chat messages, used only on the owner's computer to find highlight moments.

Where data is stored

Tokens, recordings and clips are stored only on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sent anywhere except the video uploads to the platforms the owner chose.

Sharing

No data is sold or shared with third parties. Uploaded videos are handled by the receiving platform under its own privacy policy.

Deleting data

The owner can disconnect the app at any time in each platform's settings and delete the local files. Deleting the secrets folder removes all stored tokens.
